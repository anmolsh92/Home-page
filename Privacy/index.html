PRIVACY POLICY

Your data, kept small.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers anmolshrivastava.in and the projects linked from it that use its common sign-in page. The site is run by Anmol Shrivastava as a personal portfolio.

What I collect

  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture, as shared by Google. Nothing else from your Google account.
  • If you sign up with email: your email address and a password. The password is stored only in encrypted form.
  • What you enter in a project: anything you type into one of the demo projects after signing in.

How I use it

Only to sign you in, keep you signed in across my projects, and show you your own data inside them. I don't send marketing emails, sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

Where it's stored

Sign-in details and project data are stored with Supabase, which provides the database and login service. The site itself is hosted on Vercel. Both process data only to run these services.

Your choices

You can ask me to see or delete your account and everything linked to it at any time. Email anmol.shri16oct@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and I'll handle it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
