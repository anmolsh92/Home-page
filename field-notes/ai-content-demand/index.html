Demand for AI-generated content is booming. Readiness for DIY content agents is not.

The catch

Teams still prompt outsource

Agent adoption has a long way to go. DIY hasn't kicked in the way we expected, and most teams still prefer agencies to produce their AI content.

Low risk appetite

If it falls flat, the agency owns it

Heavy bureaucracy keeps risk appetite low. When AI strips out realism and the content flops, it's easier when the risk sits outside.

Inertia

Old ways of working stick

Hands-off habit

Outside partners do the hands-on work

Cheap retries

Endless changes, last-minute saves

habit beats tooling, for now

Who's filling the gap

Two players fill the gap

Existing brand agencies and partners

They're supplying the AI-generated content, and guarding their share of marketing spend.

Lower prompting effort and tools that simplify repetitive workflows help them meet much of the demand.

Global AI content agent companies

They've started managed-service verticals of their own.

Content workflow automation players are adapting to this market reality by doing parts of the job for brands, with a focus on India and parts of Southeast Asia.

even the tool makers sell service

The silver lining

Brands want an in-house studio

Top brand agencies and marketing publications see it too. The scale is small, but companies clearly want strong in-house creative teams.

Why in-house wins
A few young interns at an early-stage company beat a marketing giant.

That only happens in-house. On-the-floor creative discussions build belonging to the brand and spark new ideas. An outsourced partner can't deliver that agility.

culture is the creative moat

D2C watch

Affluent Indians travelling abroad now love in-person stores from lifestyle and luxury brands. Brands will try to recreate that here, even through promotional stores, and agencies will play a bigger role.

The call

Both models win

Empowered in-house teams will propel adoption of agentic content workflows.

Inertia keeps plenty of room for managed services, where freelancers and small teams deliver AI content for brands.
